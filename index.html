■  Precision

Precision where it matters.

A pipe wrench works because of a small amount of play. The hook jaw rocks slightly in the frame, so turning force pulls the teeth tighter onto the pipe instead of letting them slip.

  • AAngled teeth bite in the direction of turn and release on the back-stroke.
  • BA knurled adjusting nut sets the opening with one thumb.
  • CA spring keeps the hook jaw ready to re-grip between strokes.
In use — making up a threaded joint

■  Daily work

Built for daily work.

Most joints are made in awkward places: under a sink, against a wall, overhead in a plant room. The tools are shaped for short swings, one-handed adjustment and a firm hold when you can't see the jaw.

  • —Handles sized for gloved hands.
  • —Hang holes for van racking and workshop boards.
  • —Clear part markings so the right tool is picked first time.
